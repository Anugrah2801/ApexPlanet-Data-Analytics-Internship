Key Business Findings

  • Electronics generated the highest category-wise sales.
  • Laptop recorded the highest sales among the top 5 products.
  • Patna recorded the highest sales among the displayed cities.
  • March 2025 was the strongest month in the displayed period.
  • Quantity and Unit Price show positive relationships with Total Sales.
  • Age has almost no relationship with Total Sales.

Dashboard Conclusion

Sales performance is more closely associated with product quantity and unit price than with customer age. Electronics is the leading category, Laptop is the highest-selling product among the top five, and Patna is the highest-performing city among the displayed cities.
